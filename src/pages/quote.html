What we're proposing

The general enquiry form is replaced by a quote that builds as you go. Lines arrive from the finder, product pages and colour selector, or are added here, with an indicative total ex and inc VAT. Custom sizes and sloping ceilings can be described, with a photo. The last step asks only for the details needed to reply.

Why

Every page on the site ends at the same form, which asks for a company address but not for the size, depth and quantity the FAQ says are needed to quote, so most enquiries must need a second email or a phone call before anyone can price them. This keeps a person replying to every quote, but starts the conversation with the facts.

It stops short of taking payment. Online ordering for standard sizes can follow on the same lines (roadmap stage 4) if wanted.
