What we're proposing

One template for every collar size, and later the deflectors, that holds everything a buyer needs to order correctly: photos, the full price table, a depth box that picks out the right row and price, colour and quantity, the right drawing, delivery cut-off, what fits it, and what we need from you.

Why

Today each size page is a price table and two buttons, and the deflector pages are empty. The tables don't say whether prices include VAT, the 650 page links to the 750 drawing, and four of the five sizes list the same hole size. Moving the figures into one template fixes those once and keeps them fixed.

Prices, depth bands and hole sizes are the live figures. We've treated prices as ex VAT, which needs confirming. Switch sizes with the buttons below the breadcrumb.

    What we need from you

    1. The size, or the indoor unit model number and we'll work it out.
    2. Standard or compact cassette. The finder tells you which.
    3. The depth of the gap, between 15 and 320mm.
    4. How many, and the colour if not white.

    How to measure the depth

    Diagram: ceiling line, cassette body and facia, with the gap marked
    1. Fit the cassette at its final height.
    2. Measure from the finished ceiling down to where the facia will sit, at all four corners.
    3. Give us the largest of the four. If they differ by more than 10mm, the ceiling may be sloping: add a photo with your quote.
