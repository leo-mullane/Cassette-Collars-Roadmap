Prototype 4 of 5 · RAL colour selector

What we're proposing

A colour page you can search by RAL code or name, filter by family, and act on: choose a colour and go straight to a collar in it, or apply it to every line in your quote. Each colour says plainly whether it's standard or adds time and cost.

Why

The current RAL page is a single image of a colour chart with no text, so it can't be searched, read by a screen reader, or found by anyone searching for "RAL 9005 cassette collar". Colour is one of the main reasons to buy a collar at all, and the page doesn't say what choosing one involves.

Swatches are the only colour in these greyscale prototypes, because here the colour is the content. Thirty common RAL Classic colours are shown; the full range would be loaded the same way.
