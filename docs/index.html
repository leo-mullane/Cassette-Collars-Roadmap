Five ideas for cassettecollars.co.uk

Clickable prototypes of five improvements, each working as it would on the live site. They're in greyscale on purpose, so the conversation is about what the pages do rather than how they look.

Version 1 October 2026 Greyscale prototypes Live prices, sample data where marked

The prototypes

  1. Fitment finder
    1

    Find the collar for your unit

    Search by indoor unit model number, or choose brand and range, and see the facia, the collar that fits and its price. Built from the model data on the live brand pages.

    Try: type "PLA-ZM" and pick a Mitsubishi Electric model, then choose depth and colour.

    Open prototype
  2. Quote builder
    2

    A quote that builds as you go

    Replaces the general enquiry form with line-by-line sizes, depths, colours and quantities, an indicative total ex and inc VAT, custom sizes with a photo, and five fields to send it.

    Try: change the 1000 × 1000 quantity to 10 and watch the volume discount appear, then add a custom collar for a sloping ceiling.

    Open prototype
  3. Product page
    3

    A product page that answers the questions

    One template for every size: the live price table with VAT shown, a depth box that picks out your row, colour and quantity, the right drawing, what it fits, what we need from you and the dispatch cut-off.

    Try: type a depth of 175, then 310, and see how each is priced.

    Open prototype
  4. RAL colours
    4

    Colours you can search and choose

    Search by RAL code or name, filter by family, see what a colour adds in time and cost, and apply it to a collar or the whole quote.

    Try: search "anthracite", then apply it to your quote.

    Open prototype
  5. Projects and reviews
    5

    Projects and reviews

    Installs filtered by setting and product, a place for real reviews, and the company story told once.

    Try: filter to Offices and Deflectors, then open a project and use the arrow keys.

    Open prototype

How to review

  • Everything interactive works: search, filters, the depth and price calculation, the quote and its steps, colour choice and the project viewer. Links to pages outside these five go nowhere by design.
  • Anything added to the quote follows you between prototypes for the rest of the session. The quote link sits in the bottom right corner.
  • Use the bar at the top to move between prototypes. Switch Notes on (top right) to see what each prototype proposes and why, plus notes on behaviour (yellow) and open questions for Cassette Collars (red). Notes start switched off.
  • Collar sizes, depth bands, hole sizes and prices are taken from the live site. Deflector prices, delivery, colour surcharges and discounts are sample figures.
  • Works on a phone: open this page on your mobile to check the layouts.
