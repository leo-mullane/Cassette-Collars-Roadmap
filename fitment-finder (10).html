What we're proposing

A single place to answer the first question every buyer has: which collar fits my unit? Type the indoor unit model number from the rating plate, or pick the brand and range, and the page names the facia, the collar size and the price, with one click through to choose a depth.

Why

The model data already exists. It sits on five brand pages published in October as long runs of text, which aren't linked from the menu, and on two of them the heading names the wrong brand. Held as structured data, the same information can be searched, kept up to date in one place and reused on every product page.

Brands, ranges, model numbers and facia references are taken from the live brand pages. Which collar size suits which facia is our sample logic and needs confirming.

Find the collar for your unit

Search by the indoor unit model number on the rating plate, or choose the brand and range. We'll show the collar that fits and what it costs.

    Where's the model number?

    On the rating plate inside the cassette, behind the facia, or on the original order. It usually starts with the range letters, for example FFA, PLA or AC.
