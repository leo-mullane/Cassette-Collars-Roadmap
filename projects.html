What we're proposing

A page that shows the products doing their job: installs grouped by setting and product, each with a line on the problem it solved, real customer reviews with a count, and the company's story told once and consistently.

Why

Good install photos exist but are scattered across the brand pages. The homepage has two testimonials and a claim of more than 200 clients, and we couldn't find any reviews elsewhere. It also says 15+ years where About says 30. Specifiers and facilities managers want to see a collar in a room like theirs before they ask for a price.

Project titles and descriptions are illustrative. Reviews are left as placeholders: we won't write customer quotes.

Projects

Collars and deflectors in restaurants, offices, schools, shops and hotels across the UK.
